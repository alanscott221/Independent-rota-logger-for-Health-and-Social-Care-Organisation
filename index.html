Settings, help & backup

About Rota Logger. An independent checker for the hours published on your rota system (e.g. Carista). Log each shift as you work it, then compare the weekly and period totals with the rota and your payslip. If something doesn't match, you have your own dated record to raise with your manager.

Split days. Use “Add another part of the day” for split shifts (e.g. 9–11 then 12–3). Each part can be a different service, so a day shared between two services is logged correctly.

Overtime. Hours worked in a period are compared with weekly contract hours × weeks. Over contract shows green; under shows red. Zero-hours? Set contract hours to 0 to see totals and averages instead.

Privacy. Everything stays on this device. This is a personal record, not an official timesheet. Export a backup now and then.
